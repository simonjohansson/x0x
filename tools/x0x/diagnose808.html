Blind comparison

Listen to A and B, then X. Choose whether X is A or B. The test has 16 trials, one per setting, in random order. A/B identities and X change independently each trial; answers are revealed only at the end.

This tests whether you can tell the sounds apart, not which sounds better. A low score does not prove that all differences are inaudible. No listening result has been supplied with this report.
