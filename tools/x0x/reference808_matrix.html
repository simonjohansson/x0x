Known tradeoffs in this revision

Raw waveform RMS is diagnostic, not an agreed fidelity gate. Its complete residuals are retained in the evidence. A green cell here never stands in for an unmeasured listening or harmonic check.

Measurement rules and provenance

Duration: cumulative-energy t90 and t99 over the complete 12 seconds; allowed error is 10% of the reference time or 5 ms, whichever is larger. Peak: first 500 ms. RMS level windows: attack 0–25 ms, body 25–150 ms, tail/body 150–500 ms; level limit ±2 dB. Reference level below .001 FS is a quiet diagnostic, not a pass. Existing pitch diagnostics require at least three valid cycles in both signals; limits shown are 50 cents for evolution and 20 cents for settling/quiet, with no new amplitude-based pitch qualification implied. The broad spectrum diagnostic uses the existing 20–200 ms symmetric-Hann power ratio of 150–1,000 Hz to 30–150 Hz; its meaningful-band floor is not frozen, so it has no pass/fail gate. Raw waveform residual RMS likewise has no agreed acceptance gate.

All scheduled timing is preserved. Reference ×4 undoes the audited capture routing; the fixed DSP factor remains shared across settings/CPUs and normal native volume remains downstream. No comparison-time gain, phase, delay or DC fitting, normalization, averaging or held-out use.
